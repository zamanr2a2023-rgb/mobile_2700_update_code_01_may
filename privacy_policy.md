## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TruckFix Ltd (“**TruckFix**”, “**we**”, “**us**”, “**our**”) collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you use the TruckFix mobile app and related services (the “**Platform**”).

We process personal data in accordance with UK data protection law, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

By creating an account or using the Platform, you confirm that you have read this policy.

---

## 2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to:

- **Fleet Operators** — businesses or individuals requesting vehicle repair, breakdown or roadside services
- **Mechanics** — independent mechanics, workshops and technicians providing services through the Platform
- **Company** and **Employee** users — organisations and staff using company features
- Visitors who browse onboarding screens before registering

The Platform is intended for **business use** and users must be **18 years or older**.

## 3. Data we collect

We may collect the following categories of personal and business data:

| Category | Examples | How it is collected |
|----------|----------|---------------------|
| **Account & identity** | Name, email address, password (stored securely), phone number, role (Fleet / Mechanic / Company / Employee), profile photo | Registration, login, profile screens |

| **User content** | Photos, reviews, job notes, dispute evidence | Uploaded by you |

We only collect data that is reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace, process payments, match jobs, provide support and meet legal obligations.

## 8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — request deletion (see Section 10)

To exercise these rights, contact **legal@truckfix.co.uk**. We will respond within one month.

You may also complain to the **Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)** at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## 10. Account & data deletion

You may request deletion of your TruckFix account and associated personal data at any time.

### How to request deletion

**Option A — In the app (recommended)**  
1. Open TruckFix and sign in  
2. Go to **Profile** → **Help & Support**  
3. Select **Delete my account** or contact support with the subject **“Account deletion request”**  
4. Confirm the email address registered to your account

**Option B — By email**  
Send a request from your registered email address to:  
**legal@truckfix.co.uk**  
Subject: **Account deletion request**

Include:
- Your full name
- Registered email address
- User role (Fleet / Mechanic / Company / Employee)
- Optional: reason for deletion

We will verify your identity before processing the request.

### What happens after you request deletion

- Your account will be **deactivated** and you will no longer be able to sign in
- Your profile will be **removed from public view** on the Platform
- **Personal identifiers** (name, email, phone, profile photo) will be **deleted or anonymised** within **30 days**, unless we must keep certain records longer for legal reasons

### Data we may retain after deletion

We may keep limited data where required by law or legitimate business needs, including:

| Data | Reason | Retention |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Completed job & payment records | Tax, accounting, fraud prevention, disputes | Up to **7 years** |
| Anonymised analytics | Service improvement (no longer identifies you) | Indefinitely |
| Legal correspondence | Compliance and defence of claims | As required |

Stripe may retain payment records according to its own policies. You can contact Stripe separately regarding payment data they hold.

### Deletion timeline

- **Acknowledgement:** within **5 business days**
- **Account deactivation:** within **14 days**
- **Personal data erasure/anonymisation:** within **30 days** (unless extended due to legal holds)

## Appendix — Company invitation (mobile)

Independent mechanics receive company invitations in-app (**Profile → Company invitations**). New users without an account open the invitation link, validate the token, and register as `MECHANIC_EMPLOYEE`.

**Known backend limitation:** users who already have the `MECHANIC_EMPLOYEE` role with an *inactive* company membership may fail accept/re-invite until the backend reactivates membership. The mobile app surfaces the API error message as-is and does not invent local membership or role changes.
